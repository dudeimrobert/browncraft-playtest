# Alpha 2.5.3 · Knight Inventory

- Fart Charge stun is 1 second. A red Out of range warning appears for a target beyond 9.5 world units, or if a charge stops before contact. Distant attempts do not spend Brown.
- B opens a dedicated 30-slot bag; B or Escape closes it. The pause-menu Inventory entry opens the same bag.
- Flask charges, starter equipment and recovered Guardian fragments appear as items. Hover or keyboard focus reveals name, type, details and flavor text. Remaining slots are empty.
- Existing combat and progression data remain unchanged.
