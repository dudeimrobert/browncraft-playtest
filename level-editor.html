Controls & tips

W/E/R: move, rotate, resize. F: frame selection. Shift D: duplicate. Delete: hide. Escape: cancel placement. Command/Ctrl Z: undo.

Use grid snapping for layouts, then turn it off for organic scenery. Color tints multiply textures. Moving landmarks keeps their interactions. Dock visuals do not automatically create walkable surfaces—use blocked tiles to adjust navigation.

Blocked tiles: click to toggle a cell. Red is blocked; green is traversable. Borders stay protected. Undo works for tile edits too.
