# Browncraft 2.5.2 — KNIGHT COMBAT

Extract this complete ZIP into a new folder. Do not merge with an old build.
In Terminal type `python3 ` (including the space), drag `launch.py` from this folder into Terminal, then press Enter.
The launcher opens a new localhost address and always serves its own folder with caching disabled. If your browser does not open, use the address printed in Terminal.
Confirm the title screen reads `Alpha 2.5.2 · KNIGHT COMBAT`.

Near the A’jol portal, click the portal or “Traverse the Brown” to open realm selection. No boundary travel is available. Mournwillow, Vuldross and Cindergut wait at the far ends of their realms. Their first engagement opens a short warning dialogue; defeating them awards the corresponding Guardian Fragment and awakens the return Pootal.

The launcher uses a new port; browser-local characters from an older port will not appear there. Their existing saves are not deleted.

In Weeping Fjarts, Mournwillow now telegraphs a 120-degree **Bough Sweep** for 0.86 seconds. Test the move by dodging out of its orange arc, blocking inside it, intentionally taking one hit, and standing behind Mournwillow. The arc and hitbox should disappear after the strike, death, or disengagement.

This build uses the supplied **Swamp Meadow Crossroads Tile** as A’jol’s true ground artwork and retains the complete Alpha 2.4.2 combat rebalance. Boss spectacle and additional signature attacks remain a later milestone.

A’jol’s procedural willow props have been replaced with the supplied **Fantasy X Tree 08** GLB. Each placement uses stable randomized height, width, and rotation so the grove has variety without reshuffling on every visit.

The procedural merchant figure is replaced by the supplied cardboard **Trader** illustration. It faces the camera around the vertical axis, uses keyed transparency, has shallow cardboard backing, and retains the original stall, bag, and trade interaction.
